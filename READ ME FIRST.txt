ON AIR FOR MACOS

For Apple silicon Macs running macOS 14.2 or later, one Mac per sign.

1. Unplug the sign's wall power and both LED plugs. Use a USB-C data
   cable to connect the Mac directly to the XIAO ESP32-C3.
2. Open ON AIR.app and choose ON AIR > Set Up Sign in the menu bar.
3. Select the XIAO and enter the name and password of a 2.4 GHz
   compatible Wi-Fi network. Click Install & Pair.
4. Unplug the Mac cable. Reconnect the LEDs and power the sign through
   its rear USB-C input. Allow Local Network access if asked.
5. Choose Test Light for 10 Seconds. Then try a camera preview or call.

The green dot means the sign replied and the light is off. A red
pulsing dot means the sign confirmed the light is on. Open the menu
for the sign connection, microphone, and camera status.

The app is ad hoc signed. For macOS approval guidance, see
https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

Source, build instructions, and release files:
